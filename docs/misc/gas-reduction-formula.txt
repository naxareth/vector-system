VECTOR — Batch Minting Gas Reduction Formula
======================================================================

GAS REDUCTION FORMULA (94%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Gas Reduction % = (1 - Batch Gas / Individual Gas) × 100
                  = (1 - 180,000 / 3,250,000) × 100
                  = (1 - 0.055) × 100
                  = 94.5%

BATCH COST AS % OF INDIVIDUAL (5.5%)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Batch % = (Batch Gas / Individual Gas) × 100
          = (180,000 / 3,250,000) × 100
          = 5.5%   ← well under the 20% threshold

IN PESO (₱) — Based on MetaMask: $3.11 for 500 batch mints
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Exchange rate: $1 = ₱58

  Batch (500 mints):      $3.11    = ₱180.38
  Individual (500 mints): $56.55   = ₱3,279.73

  Savings:  ₱3,279.73 - ₱180.38   = ₱3,099.35 saved

  Cost per credential:
    Batch:      ₱180.38  / 500 = ₱0.36 each
    Individual: ₱3,279.73 / 500 = ₱6.56 each

SUMMARY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Batch minting costs ₱0.36/credential vs ₱6.56/credential
  individually = 94.5% cheaper
