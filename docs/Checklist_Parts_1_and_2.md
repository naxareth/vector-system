**What procedures will be employed to ensure voluntary consent from participants?**
Participants will be fully informed of the study's purpose, the nature of the usability test, and will be required to sign a written Informed Consent Form before participating. They will be explicitly told they can withdraw at any time.

### 2. Data Retention

**If applicable, how long will data with participant identifiers be kept?**
Data with participant identifiers will be destroyed immediately after integration into the final aggregated report. The User Testing evaluation tables do not require personal names.

**How long will anonymized data be kept?**
Anonymized aggregated data will be kept for up to 1 year for academic defense and publication purposes.
