**RnD of System Related to Vector**

**1\. LinkedIn**

**How users register and set up their profile**

* Email or phone number sign-up.  
* Prompts for full name, location, industry, and profile photo.  
* Profile strength meter encourages completion (headline, about, experience, education, skills).

**How they submit experience, education, credentials**

* Manual entry form: job title, company, duration, description.  
* Education: school, degree, field of study, years attended.  
* Credentials: "Licenses & Certifications" section – manual upload of PDF or entry of credential name/issuer.  
* Skills section: list up to 50 skills, endorsements from peers.

**How employers post jobs**

* Recruiter account required (paid tier).  
* Form: job title, company, location, employment type, description, salary range (optional).  
* Screening questions: multiple choice or yes/no.  
* Option to promote job for budget bidding.

**How job matching or recommendations work**

* Two-stage: retrieval → ranking.  
* Uses member activity (clicks, applies, views) to train models.  
* "Jobs you may be interested in" based on profile keywords and past applications.  
* Recruiters see "Recommended matches" based on profile vs job description similarity.

**What the verification system looks like (if any)**

* Work email verification for company page affiliation.  
* No credential verification.  
* "Confirmed Hire" occurs when a user updates a job title after applying (inferred, not verified).

**Additional Notes (Features only)**

* InMail: direct messaging to recruiters without applying.  
* Open to Work: private toggle that signals recruiters.  
* Skill Assessments: verified skill badges (multiple choice tests).  
* LinkedIn Learning: course completion certificates (platform-bound).  
* Alumni tool: search coworkers by school/company.

**2\. Indeed**

**How users register and set up their profile**

* Email or Google sign-up.  
* Immediate prompt: "Upload your resume" – parsing extracts data to auto-fill profile.  
* Profile is minimal; resume is primary source.

**How they submit experience, education, credentials**

* Resume upload (PDF, DOC, TXT) → automatic parsing into work history, education, skills.  
* Manual editing allowed after parsing.  
* Certifications: entered as text under "Certifications" section or included in resume.  
* Skills assessments: optional timed tests with score badges.

**How employers post jobs**

* Free posting (limited reach) or sponsored (CPC or daily budget).  
* Form: job title, company, description, requirements, salary.  
* Screening questions: employer creates yes/no or multiple-choice.  
* Auto-reject feature: candidates who answer "No" are filtered out.

**How job matching or recommendations work**

* Career Scout AI: analyzes resumes and suggests missing keywords.  
* Feedback loop: every "Apply" click and hire signal trains the engine.  
* Email alerts: daily job matches based on search history and profile.  
* "Jobs near you" location-based matching.

**What the verification system looks like (if any)**

* No credential verification.  
* Phone/email verification for account creation only.  
* Indeed Assessments: skill test scores are verified internally.

**Additional Notes (Features only)**

* Indeed Apply: one-click applications without leaving the site.  
* Company reviews: users can rate former employers.  
* Salary comparison tool: crowdsourced salary data by job title/location.  
* Resume search: employers can search candidate databases directly.  
* Sponsored jobs: bidding war for top search placement.

**3\. JobStreet (SEEK-owned)**

**How users register and set up their profile**

* Email or social login.  
* Profile divided into Public vs Private visibility.  
* Elevator pitch: short summary field (strongly encouraged).

**How they submit experience, education, credentials**

* Manual entry or CV upload (parsing support).  
* Sections: work experience, education, skills, languages.  
* SEEK Pass integration: upload documents for verification (badge appears on profile).

**How employers post jobs**

* Paid packages only (ULTRA, SPECIAL, GOLD tiers).  
* Employers buy credits to post jobs and search the resume database.  
* Form: job details, required qualifications, preferred certifications.  
* SMS alerts can be sent to matched candidates.

**How job matching or recommendations work**

* Employer-driven search: employers search the resume database by keywords.  
* Candidate-driven: saved searches trigger email/SMS alerts.  
* Recommended jobs: based on profile keywords and past applications.

**What the verification system looks like (if any)**

* SEEK Pass: document upload (diplomas, certificates, ID) for third-party verification.  
* Verified credentials get a visual badge.  
* Once verified, credentials can be reused for multiple applications.

**Additional Notes (Features only)**

* SMS job alerts: push notifications for new matching jobs.  
* Salary report: interactive tool showing market rates by role/location.  
* Company follower feature: get updates from preferred employers.  
* Application tracker: see status (viewed, shortlisted, rejected).  
* Profile visibility toggle: hide from current employer by blocking specific company names.

**One-Sentence Summary per Platform**

**LinkedIn**  
A professional social network where users manually build detailed profiles and recruiters pay to post jobs with AI matching based on activity.

**Indeed**  
A high-volume job aggregator focused on resume uploads and fast applications, with AI that suggests missing keywords but no credential verification.

**JobStreet**  
An Asia-Pacific job board with paid employer posts, basic matching, and the only verification feature (SEEK Pass) among the three.
